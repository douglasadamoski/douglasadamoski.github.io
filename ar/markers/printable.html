Beating Heart AR — v0.3

Anatomical heart (NIH 3D / Human Reference Atlas) • Atria then ventricles • No server needed

Official AR.js Hiro marker (black square about 8 cm when printed at this size)
Scan to open AR
  1. Print on matte paper. Glossy paper glares and degrades tracking.
  2. Laptop with webcam: double-click heart-ar-v0.3.html.
  3. Tablet or phone: open the copy of heart-ar-v0.3.html on an HTTPS web host, e.g. douglasadamoski.github.io/ar/ (or scan the QR code if there is one).
  4. Allow camera access when the browser asks.
  5. Aim at the marker: the heart appears standing on it.
Tip: Keep the marker flat, in good even light, with its white border visible. On screen you can change the heart rate (60, 72, 100, 120 BPM), size, X-ray view (see the valves and papillary muscles inside), and heart sounds.
